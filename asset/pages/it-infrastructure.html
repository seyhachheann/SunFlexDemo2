SunFlex's storage specialization for financial institutions

SunFlex has partnerships with leading manufacturers in the world to supply storage products and solutions such as IBM, SUN, and others. We offer a wide variety of storage products and solutions for many different functions — from individual components like hard drives, RAID cards, and hard disk trays, to full archive and storage systems such as NAS, storage networks (SAN), and long-term archiving systems.

In addition, for backup system requirements, SunFlex also provides tape, autoloaders, tape libraries, and high-performance backup and restore devices — along with supporting equipment such as storage routers, storage switches, and FC cabling, giving customers flexible choices when investing in their storage systems.

To support demand for storage and backup management, we also provide attached storage tools and software that make operating system management simple and convenient — including data recovery software, device configuration, management and monitoring of all storage devices in operation, load balancing, and virtualization.
