Big Data

Big data is a combination of structured, semi-structured and unstructured data collected by organizations that can be mined for information and used in predictive modeling and other advanced analytics applications. Big data is often characterized by the 3 categories below:

  • the large volume of data in many environments,
  • the wide variety of data types stored in big data systems
  • the velocity at which the data is generated, collected and processed.

The increasing velocities and varieties of data, data flows are unpredictable, changing often and varying greatly. Data comes from so many different sources, difficult to link, match, cleanse and transform data across systems. Businesses need to connect and correlate relationships, hierarchies and multiple data linkages. Otherwise, their data can quickly spiral out of control.

Data Warehouse

A Data Warehouse is a centralized repository that collects, stores, and organizes data from multiple sources across an organization. It provides a single source of truth for reporting, analytics, and business intelligence, enabling organizations to access reliable information and make informed decisions.

Data warehouses are designed to support historical analysis, trend identification, and performance reporting by consolidating data into a structured and consistent format.

Centralized Data Storage

Consolidate data from multiple sources into a single repository, providing a consistent and reliable foundation for reporting and analysis.

Business Intelligence & Reporting

Transform data into meaningful insights through dashboards, reporting tools, and analytics that support informed decision-making.

Data Integration & Accessibility

Enable seamless access to trusted data across departments, improving collaboration and reducing information silos.

Benefits

  • Centralize data from multiple sources into a single, consistent repository.
  • Improve reporting accuracy and business intelligence capabilities.
  • Enable faster access to reliable and trusted business data.
  • Support data-driven decision-making across the organization.
  • Reduce data silos and improve information sharing between departments.
  • Enhance operational efficiency through streamlined data management.
  • Provide historical data analysis for trend identification and forecasting.
  • Support scalability as data volumes and business requirements grow.

Why it Matters

Organizations often store data in different systems, making it difficult to gain a complete view of business performance. A data warehouse brings this information together, helping teams access accurate data, generate insights faster, and support strategic decision-making.
